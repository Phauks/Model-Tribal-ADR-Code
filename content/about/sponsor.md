# Sponsorship

The code and its research are free to read. Sponsors pay to print the book, mail a copy to each nation’s office and keep the website online.

If your organization would like to sponsor the work or help fund one part of it, [get in touch through the request form](/requests?kind=question&record=sponsorship).
