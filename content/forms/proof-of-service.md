# Proof of service

**{{court.name}}**, {{nation.name}}

Form `proof-of-service`, version 0.1.0. A court may adopt or change this form under its own law. If your court has its own form for this, use the court's form.

## Before you start

- The person who gives the papers to the other party (the **server**) fills in and signs this form.
- The server must be 18 or older and **not a party to the case**. A friend or relative can serve unless the court's rules say otherwise. {{court.service_rule}}
- File this form with the clerk before the hearing.
- Court staff can explain the steps but cannot give legal advice.

## Notes for courts adapting this form

These notes are not part of the form. They explain where each part came from.

- **What the three forms share.** All three list the documents, require an adult non-party server, use a penalty-of-perjury declaration, and record personal delivery, the date and time, the place and the server's signature (comparison of the forms listed in `based_on`, read Nov. 23, 2026).
- **Who serves.** Lummi says "The server must be a nonparty who is over the age of 18" and that a "paid process server is not required." Puyallup says papers "must be served by someone who is not a party to the case."
- **Mail and email.** Little River Band and Puyallup allow certified mail and email in some cases; Little River Band accepts email service "if all parties agree." The model marks each method as allowed only where the court's rules allow it.
- **Acknowledgment.** Little River Band accepts a "Written acknowledgement of receipt of a summons & copy of complaint, dated and signed." The model includes one.
- **Failed service.** None of the three forms records a failed attempt. The model adds that box so the court can order another method; this is the project's choice.
