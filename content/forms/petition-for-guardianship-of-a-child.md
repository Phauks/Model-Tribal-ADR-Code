# Petition for guardianship of a child

**{{court.name}}**, {{nation.name}}

Form `petition-for-guardianship-of-a-child`, version 0.1.0. A court may adopt or change this form under its own law. If your court has its own form for this, use the court's form.

## Before you start

- Use this form to ask the court to name you, or someone else, as guardian of a child under 18.
- Use one form for each child, or list brothers and sisters together if the court allows.
- Attach the child's birth certificate and proof of the child's enrollment or eligibility, if you have them.
- The child's parents must get notice. If a parent agrees, ask the parent to sign the consent at the end.
- The court may order a background check of the proposed guardian and other adults in the home, and a home study by {{nation.family_services}}.
- Filing fee: {{court.fee_guardianship}}. If you cannot pay, ask for the fee waiver form.
- Court staff can explain the steps but cannot give legal advice.

## Notes for courts adapting this form

These notes are not part of the form. They explain where each part came from.

- **What the nine forms share.** All nine ask for the child's name and birth date, the child's enrollment or eligibility, the petitioner's relationship, the reason a guardian is needed, and an oath. Eight ask where the child lives, and seven name the parents, give notice to parents or interested persons, and state the petitioner is fit (comparison of the forms listed in `based_on`, read Nov. 23, 2026).
- **Relatives.** Six forms ask for relatives or other interested persons, and Nottawaseppi Huron Band requires "the minor's grandparents and nearest of adult kin." The model puts relatives in their own table because a Tribal court often looks to extended family first.
- **Emergency orders.** Six forms let the petitioner ask for an emergency or temporary order.
- **Background and home study.** Kaw requires a background check of the guardian and every adult in the home; Leech Lake and Puyallup require a study or report by the nation's family services. The model names it in `nation.family_services`.
- **Child's view.** Little River Band and Nottawaseppi Huron Band let a child 14 or older nominate a guardian. The model uses `court.child_consent_age`.
- **Indian Child Welfare Act.** None of the nine forms mentions the Act. The Act governs state court proceedings, not Tribal courts (NICWA, The Indian Child Welfare Act: A Family's Guide, 2026), so the form needs no Act notice. A state court guardianship of an Indian child needs the state's own forms.
- **Membership to file.** Miami requires the petitioner to be an enrolled member. That is a jurisdiction choice for the court's code, so the form does not ask.
