## The Land

The lead author wrote this work as a student at the William S. Boyd School of Law, University of Nevada, Las Vegas. The university stands on the traditional homelands of the Nuwuvi, the Southern Paiute people.[@unlv-land-acknowledgement-statement; @unlv-boyd-indian-nations-gaming-governance] The Las Vegas Paiute Tribe and the Moapa Band of Paiutes are Southern Paiute nations of this region today.[@unlv-land-acknowledgement-statement; @unlv-nuwuvi-history]
