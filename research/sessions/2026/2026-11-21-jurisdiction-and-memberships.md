## What was done

Task A: statute text for 18 U.S.C. 1162 and 28 U.S.C. 1360 from Cornell's Legal Information Institute; mandatory-state facts generated from those statutes for nations whose office state is Alaska, California, Minnesota, Nebraska, Oregon or Wisconsin (or whose official name includes one), with Red Lake, Warm Springs and Metlakatla (criminal only) recorded as excepted and fully retroceded nations (Menominee, Burns Paiute, Santee Sioux) given only a retroceded fact. The Federal Register API returned the post-1994 retrocession notices (Salish and Kootenai, Tulalip, Santee Sioux, Yakama, Skokomish) and 18 U.S.C. 1162(d) concurrent federal jurisdiction grants (Mille Lacs, Hoopa Valley); older notices were opened through govinfo's citation link service and read in the OCR layer. Optional-state facts cite the state statutes themselves. The ICWA designated-agent list is the May 26, 2026 notice. VAWA and TLOA facts come from NCAI and Department of Justice pages. Task B: member lists were read directly from each organization's site (curl, WordPress REST API where the page was script-built, WebFetch for USET), then matched to roster ids with a normalizing matcher plus manual overrides; every non-literal match was reviewed by hand and flagged in the note with medium confidence. Constituent bands or communities listed separately (Minnesota Chippewa Tribe bands, Te-Moak bands, Washoe communities, Passamaquoddy reservations) were attributed to the roster nation and noted.

## Review by the lead session

The Indian Child Welfare Act designated-agent facts were lowered from high to
medium confidence before the build (570 facts; the rest were already medium),
because the 2026 notice prints its table as page images and the nation names
were read from those images. The July 2025 notice (90 Fed. Reg. 30950) has a
text table that can be used to check them. The values `reassumed-jurisdiction`
and `concurrent-federal-jurisdiction-accepted` were kept: the jurisdiction
`value` field is free text described in the schema.
