# Acts of Congress behind Title 25, and the sections of both Title 25s: session notes

## What was built

`scripts/law/title_25_acts.py` has three steps.

`fetch` reads the 2024 edition of Title 25 of the United States Code from GovInfo and, for each of its 2,396 section numbers, the source credit, the note on a repealed, transferred or omitted section, the "Prior Provisions" notes and the "Short Title" notes. It gives every Act it finds there a role: the Act that enacted a section in force (the first Act in the section's source credit), the Act that enacted a former section, an Act a short-title note names, or an Act that only amended a section. Each Act is then looked up on GovInfo: the link service (`https://www.govinfo.gov/link/statute/<volume>/<page>`) answers with the granule of the Statutes at Large that holds the cited page, and the granule's MODS metadata gives its title, short titles, pages, chapter and public law number. Where the granule's chapter or public law number is not the cited one (a page can hold the end of one Act and the start of several more, and GovInfo answers with the first), the script tries the other granules that start on that page and on the 40 pages before it. It also reads every section of 25 C.F.R. from the eCFR structure. It writes `research/raw/2026-11-26/title-25-acts.json` and the review file `research/reviews/2026-11-26-title-25-acts.csv`.

`file` stores each Act's granule PDF in the project's bucket under `govinfo/STATUTE-<volume>/`, the folder the site's other Statutes at Large copies use.

`records` writes a source record for each Act (`act-<date>-ch-<n>` before 1957, `public-law-<congress>-<number>` after; kind `federal-statute`), gives an existing record of the same Act its stored copy instead of writing a second one, and writes `content/law/title-25-sections.json`, the section lists the website shows.

An Act gets a record when it enacted a section of Title 25 (in force or former), when a short-title note names it, or when its official title is about Native nations or Native people. An Act that only amended Title 25 in passing, such as the Act renaming the General Accounting Office, gets no record; the review file lists it.

Names follow Title 25's own short-title notes first ("Indian Reorganization Act", "Snyder Act"), then the name GovInfo gives the Act. An Act with no short title is named the way the Indigo Book cites it, "Act of Mar. 1, 1907", with its long title shown under the name.

## Results

468 Act citations in Title 25, resolving to 462 Statutes at Large granules. 414 Acts met the rule above (192 cited by date and chapter, 222 by public law number): 388 got new records and 26 already had one, such as the Indian Reorganization Act, the General Allotment Act and the Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act. 1,014 of the 1,180 sections in force now link to the record of the Act that enacted them; the rest come from the Revised Statutes of 1874 or from an Act listed below.

On the website, each chapter page of the Code lists its sections, each linking to its page in our stored copy of the chapter and to the Act that enacted it, with repealed and omitted sections folded below. Each part page of the regulations lists its sections, linking to the eCFR. Each Act's page lists the Title 25 sections it enacted. The Statistics page counts the sections of both titles.

## Not matched

These six Acts could not be matched to their own granule on GovInfo and have no record yet: Act of June 22, 1874, ch. 389, Act of Feb. 18, 1875, ch. 80, and Act of Mar. 3, 1875, ch. 132 (volume 18 of the Statutes at Large has two parts with the same page numbers, and GovInfo answers with the Revised Statutes); Act of May 17, 1882, ch. 163 (cited at 22 Stat. 88); Joint Resolution of June 26, 1936, ch. 851 (49 Stat. 1984); and Pub. L. 97-391 (96 Stat. 1960), whose page GovInfo files under a proclamation. Pub. L. 97-399 and Pub. L. 97-429 cite the same page; 97-429 already has a record (texas-band-of-kickapoo-act-1983). The sections credited to Pub. L. 95-561 "as added" by the No Child Left Behind Act link to that Act.
