## What was done

On Nov. 21, 2026 the profiles held 225 provision records taken from section headings in National Indian Law Library tables of contents, none of them read. Each was looked up in this order: copies the project had already captured, the copy the library's page links to, the nation's own website, then a web search.

1. **First reading.** Nine helpers (Claude Haiku) took 25 records each under the brief in [`research/reviews/briefs/2026-11-22-title-only-provisions.md`](../../reviews/briefs/2026-11-22-title-only-provisions.md). Each record got a status (read, read with a different heading or number, duplicate, not found), a one-sentence summary and a log of every place searched.
2. **Second reading.** Claude Sonnet re-read every record the first pass found, with the raw text in hand rather than a summarizing fetch. In a random sample of 12 records the first pass had marked high confidence (seed 20261122), 7 needed a fix, mostly summaries that left out how a case enters or whether an award is enforced, so the second reading was widened to all of them. Of 126 re-read records, 50 were confirmed, 67 corrected and 9 changed status.
3. **Second search.** Claude Sonnet searched again for the 80 not found, with OCR now available and leads from the first pass. It found 22.
4. **Applied** with [`scripts/review/apply_title_only_reading.py`](../../../scripts/review/apply_title_only_reading.py) on top of the lead author's decisions on the provision pre-review (pull request #98), matching records by title, citation and link because that pull request had removed records.

## Results

| Outcome | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Read and summarized; heading and number match | 88 |
| Read and summarized; heading, number or version differs (noted on the record) | 20 |
| Already removed by the pre-review decisions (pull request #98) | 60 |
| Removed here as a repeat of another record | 1 |
| Removed here as not a dispute resolution provision (an article printed as reserved) | 1 |
| Copy located but seen only through an automated summary; still title-only | 4 |
| Not found online; still title-only | 51 |
| **Total** | **225** |

Of the 60 already removed, 52 were duplicates, mostly of records read earlier, and 8 were removed as not dispute resolution. Of those 8, this reading had judged one out of scope as well (a Choctaw Nation ethics rule on former arbitrators), had marked four borderline (two Umatilla sections, a Mashantucket conciliation period and the Sac and Fox Title 5), and had not found the other three.

Each read record now has a summary, `verification: read-in-full`, the printed heading as its title, a citation to the document read and a link to it. Seventy-seven new source records cover documents the project had no record for; their rights status is fact-only (cited and linked, not quoted). Confidence is high only where a nation's own copy was read and the text is undated or dated 2021 or later; copies hosted by others and older texts are medium. Where only a later or earlier version of a code was online, the record's note says the heading differs and the review file explains how.

The 55 still unread keep `verification: title-only` and a note pointing to the nation's search log. Of those, 27 sit on sites that refused automated requests, 6 had dead links, 4 are on library pages saying the nation has not allowed full text online, and 14 were not found anywhere searched.
