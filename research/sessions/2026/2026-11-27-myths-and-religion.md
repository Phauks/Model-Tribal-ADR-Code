# Myths and religion: session notes

## What this session added

Thirty-two story records, each shown in full on the website in the printed words:

- The twelve legends of Zitkala-Ša's *Old Indian Legends* (1901) that were not yet records, from "Iktomi and the Ducks" to "The Warlike Seven," including "Iktomi's Blanket," a story about an offering made in prayer to Inyan, the great stone, and taken back.
- The twenty evenings of Charles A. and Elaine Goodale Eastman's *Smoky Day's Wigwam Evenings* (1910), each with old Smoky Day's opening words to the children of the camp, the story and its moral. They include the creation stories of the Little Boy Man ("the Adam of the Sioux," in the preface) and of the wars of Wa-kee-yan, "the Great Bird of storm and tempest," and Unk-tay-hee.

## How each text was checked

Each text was built from the Project Gutenberg transcription and checked word for word against the OCR of every scan of the printing on the Internet Archive: three scans of *Old Indian Legends* (1901) and two of *Smoky Day's Wigwam Evenings* (printed 1910 and 1912). A word of the transcription was kept only where at least one scan reads the same, and every other difference was read on the page images. Running heads, page numbers and illustration captions were set aside.

The checks found places where the transcription departs from the printing. The printing was followed in each:

- *Old Indian Legends*: "Dumfounded" (not "Dumbfounded"), "kinš, kinš" (not "king, king"), "hand's-breadth" (not "handsbreadth"), "Those bad, bad gray wolves," "surprise,—holding," "Ho! po!," "Yes! yes!" and a closing quotation mark after "You see how it is."
- *Smoky Day's Wigwam Evenings*: "whizzing sound," "gently rippling," "holding a feast" (the 1909 transcription reads "least"), "door-flap:", "door-way" and "today."

The Gutenberg transcription of *Old Indian Legends* also leaves out the marks the printing sets over letters in Dakota words and calls. Every word not in an English word list, and every short call, was read on the page images, and the marks were restored: Hĕ, Hĕ-hĕ-hĕ, Hun-hĕ, Ĕn, Ištokmus, išta, nišašapi, kinš, Manštin, Patkaša, Āhäš, Wän, Ūun, Ä-bo͞o, O͞op and Hinnú. *Smoky Day's Wigwam Evenings* prints no such marks.
