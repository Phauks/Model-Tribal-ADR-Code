# Public review timeline: session notes

The website's timeline page (/timeline) shows the path from research to public review of the model code. Its steps come from the project roadmap's gates and from the recommendation, in the Nov. 23, 2026 note on similar legal projects, to run the drafting stage the way the Uniform Law Commission runs a drafting committee.

Checking the pages changed two things in that recommendation. First, the Commission's pages say observers are invited and meetings are open, but not that observers cannot vote, so the timeline states the no-vote rule as the project's choice. Second, the Commission's line-by-line reading at its Annual Meeting is one of a committee's regular meetings, not only a final step. The timeline keeps a final reading for each division as the project's choice and cites the Commission only for the practice.

The timeline gives dates only for steps already taken. The later steps have no dates, because no document sets them. Instead the page says how long the Commission's process takes (at least two years). It also says how long the secured transactions act took: four years of drafting, then several months of debate before the Crow Nation adopted it.
