## What was done

Bulk-first method. (1) Read 25 C.F.R. § 11.100 through the eCFR API and the 2020 Federal Register rule that moved the list to the Bureau of Indian Affairs website; took the current list from bia.gov/CFRCourts. (2) Parsed the Tribal Law and Policy Institute 'Justice Systems of Indian Nations' directory into court names and linked constitutions/codes. (3) Fetched all 596 National Indian Law Library per-nation pages (1 request per second) and parsed the Tribal Code, Tribal Constitution and Contact Info blocks; then fetched the library's constitution and code table-of-contents pages to get exact titles, ratification/revision dates, and to search headings for peacemaking, mediation, arbitration and related terms. (4) Parsed the Native American Rights Fund Indigenous Peacemaking Initiative law lists (general, youth and family, other materials). (5) For government structure, fetched the University of Oklahoma Law Library collection of IRA-era constitutions and charters (351 files, including 6 PDFs) and classified each constitution by the statute it cites; added Haas (1947) Table B (main, Oklahoma, Alaska). Names were matched to roster ids by exact normalized name, then by distinctive-token overlap; every non-exact match was reviewed by hand and corrected where wrong (for example Wilton Rancheria, Gila River).
