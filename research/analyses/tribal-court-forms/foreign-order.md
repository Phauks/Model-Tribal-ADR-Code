# Foreign Order Petitions: Comparison Matrix

Nation codes used below: GTB = Grand Traverse Band; HCN = Ho-Chunk Nation; LRBOI = Little River Band of Ottawa Indians; MPTN = Mashantucket Pequot Tribal Nation (two files, counted once); NHB = Nottawaseppi Huron Band of the Potawatomi; PSA = Pueblo of Santa Ana; SUIT = Southern Ute Indian Tribe; UMU = Ute Mountain Ute Tribe (the form is headed "Court of Indian Offenses, Ute Mountain Ute Agency").

## 1. Forms read

- **foreign-order__grand-traverse-band.txt**: "PETITION AND AFFIDAVIT FOR RECOGNITION OF FOREIGN JUDGMENT" (with "ATTESTATION AND CERTIFICATION OF RECORDS"). Covers: all ("money, injunctive, declaratory, or other relief"); custody addressed in Rule 10.206(E). Standard: "full faith and credit," "reciprocal full faith and credit." Law: "GTBCR, Chapter 10"; "MCR 2.615(B)"; "[25 USC §§1901-1963]". Sworn: yes (affidavit, notary block). Fee: "$100" (also "the fee for other courts is $50").
- **foreign-order__ho-chunk-nation.txt**: "PETITION TO REGISTER AND ENFORCE A FOREIGN JUDGMENT OR ORDER." Covers: money (wages, income, liens on trust-land property). Standard: "full faith and credit and comity." Law: "HO-CHUNK NATION CONSTITUTION, ART. VII, § 5(a)"; "Rule 73"; "Wis. Stat. § 807.01(4)". Sworn: yes (oath, notary block). Fee: not stated.
- **foreign-order__little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt**: "REGISTRATION OF FOREIGN SUPPORT JUDGMENT FOR TRIBAL MEMBERS," with Michigan MC 62 and MC 202 attached. Covers: child support and arrearage; MC 62 covers money judgments. Standard: none named. Law: "MCL 691.1171 et seq."; "28 USC 1738"; "MCR 2.107(C)(3)". Sworn: yes (MC 62 affidavit, notary). Fee: not stated.
- **foreign-order__mashantucket-pequot-tribal-nation-guide.txt**: "GUIDE TO REGISTERING AND ENFORCING A FOREIGN JUDGMENT IN THE MASHANTUCKET PEQUOT TRIBAL COURT." Covers: money judgments; installment orders; wage execution. Standard: none named. Law: "TITLE 23, FOREIGN JUDGMENTS LAW." Sworn: not stated in guide. Fee: $50.
- **foreign-order__mashantucket-pequot-tribal-nation.txt**: "APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION AND ENFORCEMENT OF FOREIGN JUDGMENT" (Form 2). Covers: money (claim types: landlord/tenant, loan, property damage, personal injury, other). Standard: none named. Law: "23 M.P.T.L. ch. 1 §§ 2–3." Sworn: no; "In making this application I affirm." Fee: $50.
- **foreign-order__nottawaseppi-huron-band-of-potawatomi.txt**: "PETITION & AFFIDAVIT FOR RECOGNITION OF FOREIGN COURT ORDER." Covers: judgments and orders (not limited by type). Standard: "reciprocal recognition and enforcement." Law: none. Sworn: yes ("depose and state"; "Subscribed and sworn"). Fee: not stated.
- **foreign-order__pueblo-of-santa-ana.txt**: "PETITION FOR DOMESTICATION OF A FOREIGN ORDER." Covers: money judgments (amount due, interest, costs, fees). Standard: none named. Law: none. Sworn: verified under penalty of perjury; no notary block. Fee: not stated.
- **foreign-order__southern-ute-indian-tribe.txt**: "PETITION TO DOMESTICATE AND RECOGNIZE FOREIGN COURT ORDER" (SUIT Form 46), with affidavit. Covers: not specified ("Order/Judgment"; respondent failed "to abide by the terms"). Standard: none named. Law: none. Sworn: yes (affidavit, jurat). Fee: not stated.
- **foreign-order__ute-mountain-ute-tribe.txt**: "PETITION FOR REGISTERING A FOREIGN PROTECTION ORDER," with "Instructions for Registering a Civil Restraining Order." Covers: protection orders only. Standard: none named; registered "in the same manner as a protection order issued by this Court." Law: "Violence Against Women Act of 1994"; "Full Faith and Credit Clause of the U.S. Constitution." Sworn: yes ("swear/affirm under oath"; notary or clerk). Fee: $35 (not for domestic violence orders).

No file cites 18 U.S.C. 2265, 25 U.S.C. 1911(d), or the Full Faith and Credit for Child Support Orders Act.

## 3. Differences worth noting

- **GTB**: objection window of 28 days: "a request for a Hearing on the same within twenty-eight (28) day period" (grand-traverse-band).
- **GTB**: proof of service due in 90 days or dismissal: "the case will be dismissed by the Tribal Court and the filing fee forfeited" (grand-traverse-band).
- **GTB**: refusal ground for custody: "The provisions of the Indian Child Welfare Act [25 USC §§1901-1963], if applicable, were not properly followed" (grand-traverse-band).
- **GTB**: public-policy objection: "Is repugnant to the public policy of the Grand Traverse Band of Ottawa and Chippewa Indians" (grand-traverse-band).
- **GTB**: reciprocity requires an enacted measure: "Enacts an ordinance, court rule, or other binding measure that obligates the other Tribal Court" (grand-traverse-band).
- **GTB**: fee is internally inconsistent: "A $100 filing fee" alongside "(note: the fee for other courts is $50)" (grand-traverse-band).
- **LRBOI**: 21 days to challenge, then no further chance: "you will not be permitted any future opportunity to challenge it" (little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians).
- **MPTN**: 20-day waiting period: "There is a 20 day waiting period after proof of service upon the Judgment Debtor" (mashantucket-pequot-tribal-nation-guide).
- **MPTN**: public-policy finding: "The foreign judgment does not contravene the public policy of the Mashantucket Pequot Tribe" (mashantucket-pequot-tribal-nation).
- **HCN**: relief limited to trust land: "located on trust lands of the Ho-Chunk Nation" (ho-chunk-nation).
- **UMU**: no temporary ex parte orders and fee waiver: "Do not register a temporary ex-parte protection" and "unless you are registering a protection order involving domestic violence" (ute-mountain-ute-tribe).

## 4. Plain-language features

- **GTB**: "We cannot accept a form that is incomplete." Numbered steps list the required parts.
- **GTB**: field-level guidance: "The Tribal Court will issue its own case number, so please leave that area blank."
- **HCN**: line-by-line guide: "Enter your full, legal name on the petitioner line." Includes a plain definition: "Jurisdiction is the court's power to exercise authority over all persons and things within its territory."
- **HCN**: "Do not include these instruction sheets when you file the completed form."
- **MPTN guide**: "Submit your forms only - do not file this instruction sheet with the Tribal Court." Plus a glossary entry: "Also commonly referred to as a wage garnishment."
- **MPTN Form 2**: checkbox steps, e.g., "Step 1 - I have completed and signed Form 1."
- **LRBOI**: states what happens next: "you will be notified of the date, time and place by mail."
- **UMU**: marks required fields with "***Required" and warns: "Do not register a temporary ex-parte protection that was issued without notice to the opposing party."
