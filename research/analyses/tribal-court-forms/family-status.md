# Family status forms: comparison matrix

Source: text files in the text extracted from the private copies under `tribal-court-forms/` (see research/capture-index.csv). Quotes are from OCR-extracted text and keep its errors. Nation keys: BIA-SP = Court of Indian Offenses, Southern Plains (Anadarko, OK); Acoma = Pueblo of Acoma; Ponca = Ponca Tribe of Oklahoma; Puyallup = Puyallup Tribe; Sisseton = Sisseton Wahpeton Oyate; Wyandotte = Wyandotte Nation; Lummi = Lummi Nation; Kaw = Kaw Nation; Leech Lake = Leech Lake Band of Ojibwe; Little River = Little River Band of Ottawa Indians; Miami = Miami Tribe of Oklahoma; Nottawaseppi = Nottawaseppi Huron Band of Potawatomi.

## Part 1: Name change (7 nations)

### Forms read

- **name-change__bia-court-of-indian-offenses-southern-plains.txt**: "NAME CHANGE OF ADULT PACKET" / "PETITION FOR NAME CHANGE OF ADULT". Adult. Sworn ("duly sworn"; clerk jurat). Law: "25 CFR 11.116 and 11.611". Fee: $110.00. Notice by Publication form, hearing set by court. Attach: tribal enrollment proof (Tribal ID, CDIB or tribal letter), birth certificate.
- **name-change__lummi-nation.txt**: "NAME CHANGE PROCEDURES - ADULT" (procedures only, no petition text). Adult. No sworn form; judge swears in at hearing. Law: "Lummi Code of Laws 11.07". Fee: $50.00 or fee waiver. Publication: not stated. Attach: not listed.
- **name-change__ponca-tribe-of-indians-of-oklahoma.txt**: "Name Change of Adult" packet (instructions, petition, summons, affidavit). Adult. Sworn, notary jurat. Law: none cited. Fee: $65.00. Publication: "Name change, adoption, and other petitions/documents require publication"; $75.00 average. Attach: identification, tribal enrollment/CDIB cards, birth and death certificates.
- **name-change__pueblo-of-acoma.txt**: "PETITION FOR CHANGE OF NAME" (child). Minor. Not sworn or notarized. Law: none. Fee: $25.00 (tribal members) or $80.00 (non-tribal). Publication: not stated. Attach: none listed.
- **name-change__puyallup-tribe.txt**: "Name Change of an Adult" guide; "PETITION FOR NAME CHANGE OF AN ADULT". Adult. Sworn, notary jurat. The oath line is letter-shifted in the source; decoded it reads "certify under penalty of perjury under the laws of the Puyallup Tribe of Indians". Law: "PTC 4.08.080"; "Puyallup Tribal Code 4.16.230" (guide). Fee: $20.00, exact amount. Publication: court orders three consecutive weeks "at the Court's expense"; exemption available. Attach: photo ID showing address, enrollment proof, Statement of Address, exemption proof if any.
- **name-change__sisseton-wahpeton-oyate.txt**: "PETITION FOR CHANGE OF NAME" (parent for minor). Minor. Sworn ("Subscribed and sworn to before me"; clerk). Law: none. Fee: $50.00 filing plus $40.00 publication. Publication: "once a week for four weeks". Attach: tribal enrollment paper or tribal ID for each party.
- **name-change__wyandotte-nation.txt**: "NAME CHANGE OF MINOR CHILD PACKET"; "PETITION FOR NAME CHANGE OF MINOR CHILD". Minor. Verified under penalty of perjury; clerk or notary jurat. Law: none. Fee: $60.00; service by certified mail (USPS), Tribal PD $30, or process server. Publication: clerk issues notice; publisher charges fee. Attach: certified state birth certificate; non-petitioning parent signs Entry of Appearance and Waiver or is served.

### Differences worth noting

- **Puyallup**: "only enrolled adult Puyallup Tribal Members, regardless of location, may petition" (effective November 30, 2020).
- **Puyallup**: publication may be exempted for "a government-administered Victim or Witness Protection Program" or the Washington address-confidentiality program.
- **Acoma**: child-only form; fees differ by membership ($25.00 vs. $80.00).
- **Lummi**: "Hearings for name changes are scheduled on Mondays on the 9:00 am calendar"; requirements are in "Lummi Code of Laws 11.07".
- **Sisseton**: "PAPER WORK WILL NOT BE PROCESSED IF THE ABOVE INFORMATION" is not provided; all parties must list addresses and tribal documents.
- **Ponca**: service options list "Certified Mail ($20)", "Process Server ($40 and Up)", "Publication ($75 and Up)".
- **Wyandotte**: asks whether the petitioner is avoiding "creditors, which includes child support orders".
- **BIA-SP**: "Forms must be typed double-spaced and on letter size paper"; jurisdiction cited to 25 CFR.

### Plain-language features

- **BIA-SP**: "STEP 1: Complete forms." through "STEP 4: Submit forms, documents and filing fee," with checkboxes.
- **Lummi**: "Step 1. Complete the Petition" through "Step 4. Hearing," ending "Obtain a copy of the order from the Clerk's Office."
- **Puyallup**: "STEP 1: Read this guide and the petition carefully before completing," and a "What happens after you file your petition?" section.
- **Ponca**: "Submit ORIGINAL documents. Documents must be signed in front of the Court Clerk or a notary public."
- **Wyandotte**: "Packet contains:" list plus a fee table with office hours.

## Part 2: Guardianship of a minor (9 nations)

### Forms read

- **guardianship-minor__bia-court-of-indian-offenses-southern-plains.txt**: "GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR CHILD PACKET"; "PETITION FOR GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR CHILD". Minor. Sworn (clerk jurat); waiver notarized. Law: "25 CFR 11.116 and 11.610". Fee: $60.00 plus $25.00 service fee per parent or interested party. Notice: summons, 30 days to answer, certified mail. Attach: enrollment proof, child's birth certificate.
- **guardianship-minor__kaw-nation.txt**: "Guardianship of a Minor"; "PETITION FOR APPOINTMENT OF A GUARDIAN". Minor. Sworn; notary; statement of facts and sex-offender affidavit notarized. Law: "Oklahoma Sex Offenders Registration Act" (affidavit). Fee: "Most other filings are $50.00" (no guardianship-specific fee). Publication: "$75.00 (average publication cost)." Summons: 20 days. Attach: driver's license, birth certificates, CDIB cards, statement of facts, sex-offender affidavit, CANIS request.
- **guardianship-minor__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe.txt**: "PETITION FOR APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIAN (MINOR)". Minor. Sworn (notary or clerk). Law: "Leech Lake Band of Ojibwe, Title 6, Family Relations Code, Chapter 10." Fee: not stated. Notice: not stated. Attach: none listed.
- **guardianship-minor__little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt**: "PETITION FOR APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIAN OF MINOR". Minor. Declaration of truth; "Witnessed by: Clerk of the Court." Law: none. Fee: not stated. Notice: not stated. Attach: none listed.
- **guardianship-minor__miami-tribe-of-oklahoma.txt**: "GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR CHILD PACKET"; "PETITION FOR GUARDIANSHIP". Minor. Declared under penalty of perjury; notary jurat. Law: "laws of The Miami Tribe of Oklahoma and the State of Oklahoma" (oath, no code cited). Fee: $35.00. Notice: summons, 20 days. Attach: enrollment verification, IDs, birth certificate; Consent to Appointment and Waiver; Consent to Jurisdiction (non-Indian party); contact sheet.
- **guardianship-minor__nottawaseppi-huron-band-of-potawatomi.txt**: "PETITION FOR GUARDIANSHIP OF A MINOR". Minor. Declared under penalties of perjury; must be "notarized or witnessed by the Tribal Court Clerk, Tribal Court Administrator (TCA), or Assistant TCA." Law: "Guardianship and Conservatorship Code." Fee: not stated. Notice: interested persons receive hearing notice. Attach: separate statements required (jurisdiction, reasons); none listed as enclosures.
- **guardianship-minor__ponca-tribe-of-indians-of-oklahoma.txt**: "Guardianship of Minor" packet; "PETITION FOR GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR CHILD(REN)". Minor. Sworn, notary; Statement of Facts (Exhibit A) and sex-offender affidavit notarized. Law: none. Fee: $65.00. Publication: "$75 and Up" service option. Summons: certified mail $20, process server $40 and up. Attach: identification, enrollment/CDIB cards, birth and death certificates.
- **guardianship-minor__puyallup-tribe.txt**: "Petitioner's Guardianship of a Minor Guide"; "PETITION FOR GUARDIANSHIP OF A MINOR CHILD"; consent form "FC 10.16". Minor. Declared under penalty of perjury; notary on consents. Law: "Puyallup Tribal Code § 5.12.1180"; "PTC § 7.12.060" (guide). Fee: $20.00 (or fee-waiver motion). Notice: hearing notice per PTC § 7.12.060. Attach: Statement of Address; mandatory guardianship report; two parental consent forms.
- **guardianship-minor__wyandotte-nation.txt**: "GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR PACKET"; "PETITION FOR GUARDIANSHIP OF MINOR". Minor. Verified under penalty of perjury; clerk or notary jurat; consent form notarized. Law: "Title 25 Section 6" definitions (plan section garbled in source). Fee: $40.00 relative or $60.00 non-relative. Service: certified mail, Tribal PD $30, or process server. Attach: none listed; guardianship plan may be attached.

### Differences worth noting

- **Miami**: "In order to file in the Miami Tribe of District Court, you, the Petitioner, must be an enrolled member of the Miami Tribe."
- **Puyallup**: "The guardianship report is mandatory." Children's Services must be used unless "Court approval for an alternate agency" is granted.
- **Kaw**: "the court must have a background check for a prospective guardian and all other household members 18 years of age and older."
- **Nottawaseppi**: "must be notarized or witnessed by the Tribal Court Clerk, Tribal Court Administrator (TCA), or Assistant TCA."
- **Nottawaseppi (extended family)**: "you must list the name(s) of the minor's grandparents and nearest of adult kin."
- **Little River (kinship limit)**: "related to the minor within the fifth degree by marriage, blood, or adoption."
- **Leech Lake**: asks the court to order "Leech Lake Family Services to do a guardianship study as required by statute."
- **Wyandotte**: fees split: "Petition for Guardianship of Minor (Relative) $40.00" vs. "(Non Relative) $60.00."

### Plain-language features

- **Puyallup**: "Read this guide, and the petition, carefully in its entirety before completing the petition," then numbered steps 4.1 to 4.3.
- **Wyandotte**: "GUARDIANSHIP FLOW CHART" with steps from petition through "ANNUAL GUARDIANSHIP REVIEW."
- **Miami**: "All required fields MUST be filled in, if the field does not apply to you then insert 'N/A'. Blanks WILL NOT be accepted." Includes a process flow chart.
- **Kaw**: "Please fill out paperwork completely. Please hand write your signatures. Yellow highlighted items require you to circle a choice."
- **BIA-SP**: "STEP 1: Complete forms" through "STEP 4," plus a written flow chart for the process.
