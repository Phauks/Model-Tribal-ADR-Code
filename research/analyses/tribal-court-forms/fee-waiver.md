# Fee waiver forms: comparison matrix

Source: the 13 `fee-waiver__*` files in the text extracted from the private copies under `tribal-court-forms/`, covering 12 nations (Leech Lake has two files). Quotes are verbatim; line-broken source text is cut to one line.

## 1. Forms read

1. `fee-waiver__central-council-tlingit-haida.txt`: "Request to Waive Court Fees." Signer: party (Print Name, Signature, Date). Sworn: no; says only "true and correct," no notary. Law: none. Fee: none stated.
2. `fee-waiver__chickaloon-native-village.txt`: "Application to Waive Filing Fee," "Declaration," "Financial Declaration." Signer: requesting party. Sworn: penalty of perjury; no notary. Law: "Chickaloon Village Traditional Tribal Court Rules" (line-broken). Fee: none stated.
3. `fee-waiver__ho-chunk-nation.txt`: "Affidavit and Order to Waive Fees and Costs" (instructions printed in the same file). Signer: party, with counsel block. Sworn: "duly sworn under oath"; notary. Law: "Ho-Chunk Nation Rules of Civil Procedure, Rule 16(B)" (cited for pro hac vice). Fee: none stated.
4. `fee-waiver__hopland-band-of-pomo-indians.txt`: "Application for Waiver of Court Fees" (Habematolel Pomo of Upper Lake Tribal Court). Signer: party. Sworn: "swears or affirms"; notary or court clerk. Law: none. Fee: none stated.
5. `fee-waiver__kickapoo-tribe-of-oklahoma.txt`: "Petition to Proceed In Forma Pauperis" and "Poverty Affidavit" (instructions in same file). Signer: party. Sworn: penalty of perjury on the petition; notary block on the affidavit. Law: "Tribal Law Enforcement Code, Article 523" (petition); "Code, Article 532" (affidavit). Fee: "filing fee of $___" (blank).
6. `fee-waiver__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe.txt`: "Application for Proceeding In Forma Pauperis." Signer: applicant, signing before notary or clerk. Sworn: "Sworn/affirmed before me." Law: none. Fee: none; income table only.
7. `fee-waiver__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe-instructions.txt`: "Instructions - Waiver of Court Fees and Costs (In Forma Pauperis)." Not signed. Law: none cited. Fee: none; income table only.
8. `fee-waiver__little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt`: "Affidavit & Order Suspension of Fees/Costs." Signer: affiant. Sworn: "I swear that the information provided above is true and accurate"; no notary. Law: none. Fee: none stated.
9. `fee-waiver__lummi-nation.txt`: "Request for Filing Fee Waiver and Order - Civil Action." Signer: petitioner. Sworn: "penalty of perjury under the laws of the Lummi Nation"; no notary. Law: "Code of Laws 3.03.010(b)" (cited as "Lummi Nation Code of Laws," line-broken). Fee: "$50 filing fee."
10. `fee-waiver__nottawaseppi-huron-band-of-potawatomi.txt`: "Affidavit of Indigence & Request for Waiver of Fees in Civil Matter." Signer: requesting person. Sworn: "Under oath"; no notary block. Law: none. Fee: none stated. No judge order section.
11. `fee-waiver__pueblo-of-isleta.txt`: "Application for Waiver of Filing Fee" and "Order." Signer: none on the application; judge signs the order. Sworn: none. Law: none. Fee: none stated.
12. `fee-waiver__puyallup-tribe.txt`: "Motion to Waive Filing and Court Fees" and "Proof of Service" (same file). Signer: party; "/s/" electronic signature. Sworn: "under penalty of perjury under the laws of the Puyallup Tribe of Indians"; no notary. Law: "Puyallup Tribal Code § 5.12.1180" (both forms). Fee: none stated. No judge order section.
13. `fee-waiver__pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt`: "Petition & Affidavit and Order to Waive Filing Fees," with application packet. Signer: applicant. Sworn: "Under oath"; notary or court clerk. Law: packet names "Pyramid Lake Law & Order Code" and "Federal Rules of Civil Procedure." Fee: none stated (covers "filing and service fees").

## 3. Differences worth noting

1. **Benefit recipients skip finances.** Leech Lake: "skip to the signature line on page 2" (`fee-waiver__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe.txt`). Kickapoo: "You do not need to fill out this Affidavit" if receiving SSA benefits (`fee-waiver__kickapoo-tribe-of-oklahoma.txt`).
2. **Income cutoffs.** Hopland: "If income falls within Column C, 80% of fee waived," with Column D getting no waiver (`fee-waiver__hopland-band-of-pomo-indians.txt`). Leech Lake: 125% of the Federal Poverty Line. Lummi: "one hundred twenty-five percent or less of the" poverty level, using Washington State HHS guidelines (`fee-waiver__lummi-nation.txt`).
3. **Scope of waiver.** Pyramid Lake waives filing and service fees only: "Any request for waiver of any other" fees "must be made to the court" (`fee-waiver__pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt`).
4. **Must pay if denied.** Lummi: "pay the filing fee, before the case will be filed." Pyramid Lake: "must pay the filing fee prior to the Application being filed." Kickapoo: pay "within ten (10) days, or the complaint, petition or motion will be rejected" (`fee-waiver__kickapoo-tribe-of-oklahoma.txt`).
5. **Decision time and expiry.** Pyramid Lake: "issue an Order within seventy-two (72) hours"; "All fee waivers expire after thirty (30) days." Leech Lake instructions: "The IFP order will expire one year from the" date of the order.
6. **Outstanding fines.** Leech Lake requires a check for outstanding fines, and "make payment arrangements" before proceeding (`fee-waiver__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe-instructions.txt`).
7. **Garnishment of per capita payments.** Nottawaseppi: "the Court may garnish any per capita payments if I" fail to pay. Little River: "garnishment of per cap payments" (`fee-waiver__little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt`).
8. **Attorney tied to waiver.** Little River: "This and the attached application for a court appointed attorney is granted" (`fee-waiver__little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt`).
9. **No appeal language.** None of the 13 files describes appealing or asking reconsideration of a denial.
10. **No oath or signature on application.** Pueblo of Isleta's application has no signature or oath line; only its order has a judge signature (`fee-waiver__pueblo-of-isleta.txt`).

## 4. Plain-language features

- Central Council: "If you are getting public assistance, are a low-income person" opens the form. Its example: "Please provide paystubs from employer or" (`fee-waiver__central-council-tlingit-haida.txt`).
- Leech Lake instructions: "The information included in the application will be used for the sole purpose of determining indigent" and "Do not sign the form until you" (sign before the clerk or notary) (`fee-waiver__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe-instructions.txt`).
- Leech Lake instructions, Step 2: "Complete the case heading for the Order Denying" (`fee-waiver__leech-lake-band-of-ojibwe-instructions.txt`).
- Pyramid Lake: "DO NOT USE WITE-OUT or any other fluid/tape on the forms." Also defines pro se: "Latin for “on one’s own behalf”" (`fee-waiver__pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt`).
- Chickaloon: "multiply your weekly gross pay by 4.3" gives pay-frequency conversions; "If child support is at issue, complete the DR305" points to a separate worksheet (`fee-waiver__chickaloon-native-village.txt`).
- Ho-Chunk: "For childcare or child support, please draw a" circle; "Do not include these instruction sheets when you file the completed form." (`fee-waiver__ho-chunk-nation.txt`).
- Kickapoo: "Fill in the names of the plaintiff and defendant in the heading" exactly as in the complaint; "Fill in any amounts related to the type of property indicated." (`fee-waiver__kickapoo-tribe-of-oklahoma.txt`).
- Hopland: "Answer the questions carefully: you could face punishment and prosecution for perjury." (`fee-waiver__hopland-band-of-pomo-indians.txt`).
