# Protection order petitions: comparison matrix

Short names: BIA-SP (Court of Indian Offenses, Southern Plains), Kaw, Little River (LRBOI), Mescalero, Miami (MTOK), Puyallup, Pyramid Lake, Southern Ute, Tanana, Tulalip. Puyallup's guide is partly font-encoded; quotes marked "(decoded)" were recovered by character shift and may contain errors.

## 1. Forms read

- **bia-court-of-indian-offenses-southern-plains.txt**: "PETITION FOR PROTECTIVE ORDER." Emergency ex parte or regular order after hearing. Petitioner: family, household, or dating relationship. Sworn: yes. Law: "25 CFR 11.116 and 11.1202." Fee: "filing fee of $85.00" (instructions); "There are no fees for filing" (guide).
- **kaw-nation.txt**: "PETITION FOR EMERGENCY TEMPORARY PROTECTIVE ORDER." Emergency ex parte only, when court is closed. Grounds: physical harm or threat. Petitioner: listed relationships; enrolled Kaw member or 3+ months in Kaw jurisdiction. Sworn: yes. Law: none. Fee: none stated.
- **little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt**: "PETITION FOR PERSONAL PROTECTION ORDER (DOMESTIC RELATIONSHIP)." Ex parte or with hearing. Petitioner: married, cohabiting, child in common, or dating; one party enrolled and incident on Tribal property. Sworn: no oath on petition. Law: "LRBOI DV Ordinance," "MCL 600.2950," "MCR 2.105(A)." Fee: none stated.
- **mescalero-apache-tribe.txt**: "Petition for Protection Order," with checklist, affidavit, and emergency motion. Emergency motion; no temporary/final split. Petitioner: enrolled member in Mescalero jurisdiction; spouse, ex, boy/girlfriend, family. Sworn: no oath, "true statement" language only. Law: none. Fee: none; mailing cost collected.
- **miami-tribe-of-oklahoma.txt**: "PETITION FOR PROTECTIVE ORDER" (MTOK District Court). Ex parte (B) or final after hearing (A). Grounds: physical harm, threat, harassment, stalking. Petitioner: BIA-SP's relationship list plus "Victim of Rape." Sworn: yes. Law: none. Fee: none charged "except if the Court finds" frivolous filing.
- **puyallup-tribe.txt**: "PETITION for Protective Order" (Temporary Order Requested), in a packet with guide, WA confidential form, and law enforcement sheet. Emergency ex parte, temporary, final. Petitioner: relationship list incl. dating; member, employee, or none. Sworn: yes, under "Puyallup Tribe of Indians" law. Law: "18 USC § 2265(a)," "Puyallup Tribal Code" (decoded), "RCW 26.23.050." Fee: guide says no fee to serve an order (decoded); none stated on petition.
- **pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt**: "APPLICATION FOR ORDER FOR PROTECTION AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE." Temporary (ex parte) and extended orders. Domestic violence only. Petitioner: the victim must file. Sworn: yes, plus affidavit. Law: "Pyramid Lake Law & Order Code" 3.09.700 to 3.09.707. Fee: "NO FILING FEES REQUIRED."
- **southern-ute-indian-tribe.txt**: "VERIFIED COMPLAINT FOR PROTECTION ORDER" (SUIT Form #38). Civil protection order with emergency hearing request; no ex parte box. Petitioner: "threat of imminent danger." Relationship not asked. Sworn: yes, notary block. Law: none. Fee: none stated.
- **tanana-chiefs-conference.txt**: "PETITION FOR A TRIBAL COURT PROTECTIVE ORDER" (Alaska). Ex parte for 20 days; long-term order after notice. Petitioner: tribal membership of each party; no relationship checklist. Sworn: statement of truth before Tribal Court. Law: "Tribal Law & Order code" (unfilled placeholder). Fee: none stated.
- **tulalip-tribes.txt**: "Petition for Order for Protection - Domestic Violence" (No. TUL-CV-PO). Emergency temporary up to 14 days; hearing order; option for "longer than one year." Petitioner: victim, minor, vulnerable adult, or child protection agency. Sworn: yes, under Tulalip and Washington law. Law: "Tulalip Tribal Code §4.25.560," "RCW §9.41.040," "18 U.S.C. § 2265," "18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(8)." Fee: none stated.

## 3. Differences worth noting

- **Duration**: Tanana ex parte "in effect for 20 days"; Kaw "until the close of business on the next day the Court is open"; Tulalip "up to 14 days."
- **Permanent length**: Pyramid Lake "not less than Three (3) years"; Tulalip allows "Remain Effective longer than one year."
- **Who may file**: Pyramid Lake "you can NOT file on behalf of someone"; BIA-SP, Kaw, Miami, and Little River allow filing for minor family members or through a next friend.
- **Non-family harassment**: Puyallup guide, "You do not have to be in a family relationship to seek this type of order" (decoded). Pyramid Lake is domestic violence only.
- **Jurisdiction**: Little River requires "an enrolled citizen... AND at least one incident occurred on Tribal property"; Kaw requires residence "for more than three (3) months."
- **Non-member respondents**: Tulalip, "If you are NOT a member of a federally-recognized Indian Tribe, the Tulalip Criminal Code may apply or Washington State law may apply."
- **Exclusion**: Tanana, "Respondent is excluded from the village"; children "may not be removed from the State of Alaska except."
- **Cultural sentencing**: Tanana lists "Circle Sentencing," "Parenting Classes/Elder Counseling," and "Restitution through Subsistence."
- **Mutual orders**: Pyramid Lake, "The Court will NOT grant mutual order for protection."
- **Minor respondents**: Miami, "Order Defendant, who is a minor, to leave the residence," ages 13 to 17.

## 4. Plain-language and safety features

- **BIA-SP**: "These standard instructions are informational purposes only and do not constitute legal advice." Also "Court Clerks CANNOT provide legal advice" and "contact a Tribal Domestic Violence Program" (phone 405/247-9495 listed).
- **BIA-SP, service**: "Do not try and serve the defendant in person with the papers yourself."
- **Puyallup**: "Although it is not required, the Plaintiff should carry a copy of the protective order at all times." Also "You may ask the Court to keep your address confidential if the Respondent does not know where you live or work."
- **Little River**: "If you want your address and telephone number to be kept from the other party, do not write your address here."
- **Tulalip**: "You have a right to keep your residential address confidential." Also "You Can Be Arrested Even if the Person or Persons Who Obtained the Order Invite or Allow You to Violate the Order's Prohibitions."
- **Southern Ute**: "STOP: IF YOU CHECK BOX NUMBER 7, DO NOT FILL IN YOUR ADDRESS AND TELEPHONE NUMBER."
- **Mescalero**: "The Respondent could become violent when he/she is made aware of this Petition."
- **Pyramid Lake**: "COUNSEL IS ALWAYS RECOMMENDED FOR LEGAL MATTERS."
- **Kaw**: "(Note: This form is for use when the Court is NOT open for business)," with court phone "(580) 269-2552."
