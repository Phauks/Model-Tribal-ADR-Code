# Civil case forms: comparison matrix

Scope: 16 files in the text extracted from the private copies under `tribal-court-forms/` (complaint 6, answer 3, proof of service 3, small claims 4). Texts are OCR output with errors; quotes are copied from the files. BIA-SP is the BIA Court of Indian Offenses, Southern Plains. Counts are nations.

## Complaint and summons

### Forms read
- civil-complaint__bia-court-of-indian-offenses-southern-plains.txt: title "CIVIL COMPLAINT". Sworn: yes (perjury verification, clerk/notary jurat). Law: 25 CFR 11.116 and 11.503. Fee: $85.00. Time: summons gives 30 days.
- civil-complaint__keweenaw-bay-indian-community.txt: "CIVIL COMPLAINT". Sworn: yes (notary jurat). Law: none; guide cites "Title 30 of the Tribal Code". Fee: $40.00. Time: default affidavit after 21 days (on reservation) or 28 days (off reservation or certified mail).
- civil-complaint__little-river-band-of-ottawa-indians.txt: "COMPLAINT" with "SUMMONS". Sworn: no. Law: MCR 1.109(D), MCR 2.102(B), MCL 600.8035. Fee: none stated. Time: "YOU HAVE 28 DAYS".
- civil-complaint__oneida-nation.txt: "ONEIDA JUDICIARY-TRIAL COURT CIVIL COMPLAINT". Sworn: complaint no; affidavit of service yes. Law: "Rules 153.5-6". Fee: $50.00. Time: serve within 30 days of filing; proof within 10 days; respondent has "twenty (20) days".
- civil-complaint__puyallup-tribe.txt: "CIVIL COMPLAINT/PETITION" (packet with guide). Sworn: perjury certification, no notary. Law: PTC 4.08.080; Puyallup Tribal Code § 5.12.1180. Fee: $20 (guide). Time: clerk certified-mail service "takes a minimum of 30 days".
- civil-complaint__pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt: "CIVIL COMPLAINT" (packet). Sworn: yes (affidavit "first duly sworn"; notary lines). Law: P.L.L.O.C. § 3.3.204. Fee: $30.00 filing; $15.00 service inside reservation. Time: respondent 20 days, or "(20) business days to respond" on summons.

### Differences worth noting
- Oneida, non-member consent: "Non-Indian and consent to the jurisdiction of the Oneida Judiciary." (civil-complaint__oneida-nation.txt)
- Pyramid Lake, residency: "must be a resident of the reservation for at least six (6) weeks prior to filing the complaint" (civil-complaint__pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt)

- Pyramid Lake, conflicting time: "(20) business days to respond from the date of service" on summons, versus "twenty (20) days" in instructions (civil-complaint__pyramid-lake-paiute-tribe.txt)

### Differences worth noting
- Comanche, reservation of time: "a defendant who files a reservation of time waives the following defenses" (civil-answer__comanche-nation.txt)
- Comanche, 35-day option: "the plaintiff may elect to require the answer to be served within thirty-five days" (civil-answer__comanche-nation.txt)
- Comanche, unanswered averments: "Averments which are not specifically denied, except those pertaining to the amount of damages, are deemed admitted." (civil-answer__comanche-nation.txt)
- Lummi, default: "may ask the Court to enter an order granting the relief requested, without any further notice to you" (civil-answer__lummi-nation.txt)
- Puyallup, address form: "you must complete a Statement of Address form" (civil-answer__puyallup-tribe.txt). No counterclaim section.

## Small claims

### Forms read
- small-claims__fond-du-lac-band.txt: "SMALL CLAIM COMPLAINT AND AFFIDAVIT" (with instructions and Ordinance #01/09). Sworn: yes (verified; notary jurat). Law: Ordinance #01/09 (§ 101 cites 25 U.S.C. § 476). Fee: $20 filing; $10 per extra defendant; $20 counterclaim. Dollar limit: $5,000. Time: counterclaim within 21 days of service; claim within one year (§ 302).
- small-claims__grand-traverse-band.txt: "AFFIDAVIT AND CLAIM, Small Claims" (Form DC 84). Sworn: yes ("Subscribed and sworn to before me"). Law: MCL 600.8401 et seq.; MCR 4.302; MCR 4.303; 50 USC 3931. Fee: not stated (blank). Dollar limit: $7,000. Time: serve "no later than 7 days before the hearing date".
- small-claims__kaw-nation-instructions.txt: "INSTRUCTIONS FOR FILING A SMALL CLAIMS CASE" (no form in file). Sworn: not stated. Law: none. Fee: $50.00 filing; $50 tribal police service; $10 certified mail; $75 publication. Dollar limit: none. Time: 20 days to answer after service.
- small-claims__wyandotte-nation.txt: "SMALL CLAIMS AFFIDAVIT" (packet with instructions and order). Sworn: yes ("being duly sworn"; notary). Law: none. Fee: $75.00 filing; $30 tribal police service. Dollar limit: none. Time: hearing "seven (7) days after service hereof, whichever is the latter".

### Differences worth noting
- Kaw, lawyers allowed if registered: "must be registered to practice law with the Kaw Nation Bar Association" (small-claims__kaw-nation-instructions.txt)
- Fond du Lac, lay spokesperson: "spokespersons who appear at no charge and are immediate family members of a party" (small-claims__fond-du-lac-band.txt)
- Fond du Lac, one-year limit: "No claim may be filed more than one (1) year after the date that the plaintiff knew or should have known" (small-claims__fond-du-lac-band.txt)
- Grand Traverse, mediation: "you cannot resolve the dispute through mediation or other means" (small-claims__grand-traverse-band.txt)
- Grand Traverse, waivers: "you give up the right to an attorney, to a jury trial, and to appeal the judge's decision" (small-claims__grand-traverse-band.txt)

## Plain-language features

- Numbered steps and filing checklists: BIA-SP, Oneida, Kaw, Pyramid Lake.
- "Not legal advice" and "clerks cannot advise" warnings, in BIA-SP, Comanche, Kaw and Wyandotte.
- Retype rule: BIA-SP ("The forms must be retyped") and Comanche ("IT MUST BE RE-TYPED").
- Plain consequences: "YOU HAVE 28 DAYS" (Little River); "judgment by default can be rendered against you" (BIA-SP).
