# Media research

Research on videos, sound recordings and pictures the website could use in its Picture, Video and Audio blocks, with each source's reuse terms and cultural-sensitivity concerns. Nothing listed here is on the website until a person checks its rights and, where the survey says so, asks the rights holder or the nation.

- [Media source survey](2026-11-23-media-source-survey.md) (Nov. 23, 2026): sources by category, license classes, a ranked top ten, and questions that need a person.
- [Per-nation pictures recommendation](2026-11-23-per-nation-pictures-recommendation.md) (Nov. 23, 2026): why the project should not search for a picture of each nation, and what to do instead.

Media files are never kept in this repository. Copies the project is allowed to keep go to the private bucket through the upload path, with a credit and a removal link.
