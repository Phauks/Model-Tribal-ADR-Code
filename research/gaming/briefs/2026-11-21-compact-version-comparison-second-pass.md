# Brief: check how a compact's dispute resolution clause changed (second pass)

Each item pairs an EARLIER version of a Class III tribal-state gaming compact (between a Native nation and a state, 25 U.S.C. § 2710(d)) with a LATER document: an amendment to it, a replacement or restated compact, or Secretarial Procedures. A small model read excerpts and labelled how the process for resolving disputes between the nation and the state changed; an audit found its "added" labels were all wrong (it said "added" when the earlier compact already had a dispute clause and the later document only added a narrow step, a routing rule, or an appendix process) and about half its "changed" summaries misdescribed the earlier text.

You have the full extracted text of both documents (paths in the item; OCR errors possible). For each item:
1. Find the dispute resolution provisions in both texts (grep -n -i "dispute\|arbitrat\|mediat\|meet and confer" FILE, then read the surrounding lines). For an amendment, check which provisions it amends; an amendment that does not touch the dispute clause leaves it "unchanged".
2. Decide the change, one of: "added" (only if the EARLIER text has no process for disputes between the nation and the state at all), "removed", "changed" (the process differs: steps, deadlines, rules or forum, whether arbitration binds, which courts, scope of disputes covered; also a new step or a new process for one kind of dispute added to an existing clause), "reworded" (words differ, process the same), "renumbered", "unchanged", "not-compared" (the text cannot settle it, e.g. a scan with no text or a missing section).
3. Write one or two neutral sentences saying what changed, naming the steps on each side (e.g. "Adds non-binding mediation under CPR procedures before arbitration and limits court review to 9 U.S.C. § 10 grounds; the earlier compact went from 30 days of negotiation straight to binding AAA arbitration."). Say "the later compact provides"; never call a change better or worse. Use "Native nation" or the nation's name, not "the tribe", in your own words.
4. Give an exact quotation (10 to 40 words, copied character for character from the file) from each side that shows the rule, or null.

Use only the texts, never outside knowledge. Output for your part file a JSON list of {"pair", "change", "summary", "earlier_locator", "later_locator", "earlier_quote", "later_quote", "first_pass_verdict" ("right", "partly" or "wrong"), "note"} written to /tmp/claude-0/work/histories/second/answers-N.json (N = your part number). Write the file every 10 items so work is not lost. Check it parses with python3. Do not edit any other file and do not post or send anything. Report back in two sentences: how many items, and counts of first_pass_verdict.
