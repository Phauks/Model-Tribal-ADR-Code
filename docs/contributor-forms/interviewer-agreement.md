## 2. You agree to

- ☐ Follow [contributions.md](../contributions.md) and use only approved forms.
- ☐ Contact a nation's community through its offices first.
- ☐ Check for the nation's research code or review board before any interview.
- ☐ Explain the forms before recording.
- ☐ Record only with the consent of everyone present.
- ☐ Stop when asked, and never press for ceremonial or restricted knowledge.
- ☐ Store everything in the project's private storage only, and delete your own copies once uploaded.
- ☐ Keep contributors' names and unpublished material confidential, during and after your work with the project.
- ☐ Report any mistake to the lead author at once, such as a lost recording.
