# Interviewer agreement

Form `interviewer-agreement`, version 0.1.0 (draft).

For anyone other than the lead author who interviews people or collects
material for the Model Tribal ADR Code project: co-authors, volunteers,
students or community members. Modeled on the separate interviewer release
used alongside narrator releases in oral history projects, such as the
Library of Congress Veterans History Project field kit.

## 2. What you agree to do

- ☐ Follow [contributions.md](../contributions.md) and use only the current
  approved forms.
- ☐ Contact a nation's community through its own offices first, and check
  whether the nation has a research code or review board before interviewing.
- ☐ Explain the forms fully before recording, and record nothing without the
  narrator's consent and the consent of everyone recorded.
- ☐ Stop when the narrator asks, and never press for ceremonial, restricted
  or community-held knowledge.
- ☐ Store recordings, notes and forms only in the project's private storage,
  delete copies from your own devices once uploaded, and never post them
  anywhere else.
- ☐ Keep contributors' identities and unpublished material confidential, now
  and after you leave the project.
- ☐ Report any mistake, such as a lost recording or something recorded
  without consent, to the lead author at once.

## 3. Your own contribution
