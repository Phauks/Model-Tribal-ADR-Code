**About the project.** The Model Tribal ADR Code describes how Native nations resolve disputes and offers model laws they may choose to use. It is published on a website and in a book. {{project.lead_author}} leads it, is a law student, and is not Native American. The project is not a business and does not pay for contributions. Its rules on nations' control of information are in its [charter](../charter.md).

## 6. Changing your mind

You can ask us to fix, change, hide, delay or remove your material at any time. Use [Change or withdrawal](change-or-withdrawal-request.md), or just tell us. We hide it within 7 days, then act on your request.

Some things can't be undone. Books already sold stay sold; later printings leave the material out. Copies made under an open license, or saved by web archives, may remain. We will ask the archives to remove theirs.

## 7. Our promises

- We use your material only as you chose.
- We never publish your contact details.
- We keep your items in the project's private storage.
- You see any transcript and every quote before we publish.
- We tell you when your material is published.
- We follow your nation's research rules.
