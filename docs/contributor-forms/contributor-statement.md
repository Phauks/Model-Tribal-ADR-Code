This statement goes with everything you give the Model Tribal ADR Code
project that is not already published. It records what you are giving, what
you allow, and how you want to be credited. Please read it all. Ask us about
anything that is unclear. You can say no to any part.

**About the project.** The project collects how Native nations resolve
disputes, including peacemaking, mediation and Tribal courts, and writes model
laws and commentary that nations may choose to use. It is led by
{{project.lead_author}}, a law student who is not Native American, and is
published on a website and in a book. It is not a business and does not pay
for contributions. Its rules on Native nations' authority over information are
in its [charter](../charter.md).

## 6. Changing your mind

You can ask us at any time to correct, change, hide, delay or remove what you
gave, with the [request form](change-or-withdrawal-request.md) or by telling
us any way you like. We will hide it within 7 days while we act on your
request. Please understand what we cannot undo:

- printed books already sold cannot be recalled, though later printings will
  leave it out;
- copies others made under an open license, or that archive services saved,
  may remain, though we will ask archives to remove them.

## 7. What the project promises

- To use your contribution only as you chose above.
- To keep your contact details private and never publish them.
- To store your items securely, in the project's private storage only.
- To send you any transcript and every quotation before publication.
- To tell you when something you gave is published.
- To follow your nation's research rules where they apply.
