# Contributor forms

**Status:** drafts, version 0.1.0, for review by the lead author and the
project's advisors. Not legal advice. Do not use any form with a contributor
until it is approved, and check the nation's own research rules before any
interview ([contributions.md](../contributions.md#ethics-review)).

These are the project's own forms for material that reaches it from a person
rather than from a published source. How they fit together, and the filing
each one goes into, is specified in [contributions.md](../contributions.md).
They are different from `content/forms/`, which will hold model forms a nation
may adapt for its own dispute resolution (CC0).

## Rules for every form

- **Versioned.** Each form prints its id and version at the top. A filing
  records the version signed, and old versions stay in git history, so the
  exact words a person agreed to can always be shown. Any change to wording
  that changes what a person agrees to is a new minor version (project rule).
- **Plain language.** Written to the [style guide](../style-guide.md#2-plain-language):
  short sentences, active voice, no unexplained legal terms.
- **Read aloud if wanted.** Every form can be read to the person and agreed to
  on a recording ([signatures](../contributions.md#signatures)).
- **A copy for the signer**, always, on paper or by email as they prefer.
- **Nothing is required except what the form marks required.** A person can
  leave any other answer blank.
- **The narrowest permission wins.** If two forms in a filing disagree, the
  project follows the more protective one and asks.
- **Placeholders** in double braces, such as `{{filing.identifier}}`, are
  filled in by the project before the form is given out.
